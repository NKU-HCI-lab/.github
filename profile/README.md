# Welcome to NKU's HCI Lab!
The Human-Computer Interaction Lab explores innovative ways to understand and enhance interaction between humans and computers. Through interdisciplinary research, we analyze and improve user experiences, interface design, and usability across various domains.

If you are not sure where to start, visit our [**official website**](https://cprnhl.com/hcilab) and read this document.

We also have:
- an [**official YouTube channel**](https://www.youtube.com/@NKU-HCIlab) where we publish videos about our research
- a [**private repository**](https://github.com/NKU-HCI-lab/restricted_hci-lab-resources) accessible only to lab members

---
## Research areas
Our research focuses on:
- User eXperience analysis and design
- Artificial Intelligence (particularly LLMs)
- Assistive, inclusive, and sustainable technology

Check out the [list of our publications](./publications/_index.md)

## Joining the HCI Lab

### Why join?
1. ***Learn and work on interesting topics***: no matter what we achieve together, you will learn something new and meaningful.
2. ***Deliver results***: we have a goal-oriented mindset that will provide you with meaningful personal and professional accomplishments. 
3. ***Connect and thrive***: we work professionally, but in a relaxed environment.

Have any questions? Good! Read our [Frequently Asked Questions page](./pages/frequently-asked-questions.md).

### What's expected from HCI Lab members
The minimum expected weekly commitment is as follows:
- *Research project*: 15 hours (full-time researcher) or 5 hours (part-time researcher)
- *Professional development*: 2-3 hours
- *Weekly lab meetings*: 1-2 hours
- *Other activities*: 1 hour

### How to join
First, you must **fill out the [member form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/xT9tW1iZkF)**.

After you get accepted into the lab, you:
1. Should enroll in a research course: wait for further instructions from me
2. Will be assigned to a team/project based on your interests
